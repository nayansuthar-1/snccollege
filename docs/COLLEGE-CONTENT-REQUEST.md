### B. For Principal & College Management

1. **Leadership Messages & Names**
   - **Chairman / Founder:** Exact name spelling (**Prashant Choubisa** or **Prasshant Choubisa**), official designation, 1 passport-style photograph (clean background), and message text (150–300 words).
   - **Principal:** Full name, educational qualifications, 1 photograph (clean background), and message text (150–300 words).

2. **Affiliation & Regulatory Facts**
   - **NAAC Status:** Is the college currently NAAC accredited? If yes, provide Grade and Year. If in process or not accredited, please confirm so no incorrect claim appears.
   - **AISHE Code:** Exact All India Survey on Higher Education code for SNC College.
   - **University Affiliation:** Affiliation letter number or confirmation for Govind Guru Tribal University (GGTU), Banswara.

3. **Staff Directory (Excel Sheet)**
   - List of all teaching and non-teaching staff members:
     - Full Name
     - Designation (e.g., Assistant Professor, Lecturer, Lab Assistant, Clerk)
     - Department (Arts / Science / Administration)
     - Highest Qualification (e.g., M.Sc., Ph.D., NET)
     - Total Experience (in years)
     - Passport-style photo of each faculty member (optional but recommended)
